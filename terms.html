8. Device Permissions

To enable certain features, LifeMirror requests the following Android permissions. All permissions are optional and can be revoked at any time in your device settings.

  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage Access): Required for App Auto-Tracking. Detects which app is active on screen to automate session timers. Processed locally — not transmitted to servers except as part of aggregated session summaries when cloud backup is enabled.
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION: Detects physical activities (walking, running, still) to trigger context-aware modes. Processed locally on your device.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE: Detects call start/end to automatically log call duration. LifeMirror does not access, record, or transmit phone numbers, call logs, or conversation content.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & NOTIFICATION: Required to keep tracking sessions running when the app is in the background and to deliver timely reminders.

Revoking any permission will disable the corresponding feature. The App's core manual tracking functionality remains available without any special permissions.
